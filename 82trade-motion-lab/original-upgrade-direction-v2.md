# 82Trade Original Site Upgrade — Direction Lock V2

## North Star
**Original 82Trade conversion site × Kaiyn interaction density × Coinbase product response × Apple motion quality.**

This is **not** an Apple/Coinbase brand-site rebuild.
The original site owns information architecture and conversion truth. External references only donate interaction and presentation primitives.

## What stays
- black / cyan 82Trade identity
- current content truth and outbound links
- market analysis, public courses, community
- real member faces and course thumbnails
- Discord / LINE / YouTube / OKX / Instagram entry points
- mobile readability and reduced-motion fallbacks

## What changes
- homepage becomes a full conversion landing page
- more visible content density, less keynote-like empty space
- every important object has a small response state
- hero social proof is tactile
- cards reveal CTA on hover
- platform rail has motion but pauses on hover
- onboarding is explained on-page instead of hidden
- section backgrounds use structural detail, not empty black

## Donor assignment
### 82trade.com
Source of truth for:
- brand assets
- content
- links
- community flow
- learning propositions

### Kaiyn
Borrow:
- direct top-to-bottom conversion sequence
- social-proof placement
- partner/platform rail
- card push-up CTA behavior
- visible onboarding steps
- dense dark composition
Do not copy:
- trading education copy
- exchange/KYC business flow
- branding or visual assets

### Motion Primitives
Borrow mechanism:
- Magnetic: low-amplitude CTA attraction
- Tilt: one high-value media object, not every card
- In-view: restrained section reveal

### Coinbase / Apple
Use as quality references:
- Coinbase: product-card response, border/light state, compact product clarity
- Apple: easing, transform restraint, object weight, no cheap bounce

## Scene budget
1. Hero — Story/Trust, M1
   - Signature Moment: community face fan + focused member lift/scale.
2. Platform rail — Trust, M0
3. Learning cards — Story/Utility, M0
4. Onboarding — Trust/Utility, M0

No M2 on homepage. 3D Reading remains an optional later module, not the homepage architecture.

## Conversion flow
Hero value proposition
→ social proof
→ platform / channel proof
→ what you can learn
→ how to start
→ join community / contact support
